Official Competition Rule: Address the Chairperson!

All speakers must address the Chairperson and audience in their introduction (e.g., "Mr Chairperson, adjudicators, teachers, parents, and fellow Year 5 students..." or "Madam Chairperson...").

1. Rhetorical Question

"Have you ever wondered what the world would sound like if we all paused to listen?"

2. Surprising Fact

"Did you know that over 300 million tonnes of plastic are produced every single year?"

3. 'Imagine' Scenario

"Imagine a tiny garden where the smallest creature works silently to save our planet..."

4. Inspiring Quote / Story

"St. Francis of Assisi once said, 'Start by doing what's necessary; then do what's possible...'"

Step 3 of 4

Build Your Arguments (PEEL)

Adjudicators look for varied, interesting, and topic-related content!

P = Point State your main argument clearly.
E = Explain Explain why this matters or how it works.
E = Example Give a real-life fact, quote, or story.
L = Link Link back to your speech topic.
Step 4 of 4

Effective & Memorable Conclusion

Adjudicators look for an appropriate, powerful finish that leaves an impact.

Conclusion Guidelines:

  • Signal the end: "In conclusion..." or "To summarize..."
  • Summarize main points: Briefly remind the audience of your key ideas in fresh words.
  • Final Thought / Call to Action: End with a strong message, question, or inspiring closing statement.
  • Formal Thank You: Conclude politely with "Thank you."
